      Drive the S8

      Hold ↑ for power and ↓ for brake. Space toggles the emergency brake, D toggles the doors and C switches between heading-up and north-up. The driving buttons also work with touch.

      Stop within 30 m of the marker, open the doors and wait for the 15-second dwell. Close the doors before departure. Use Wait for departure to skip a scheduled hold. Clock speed scales motion, dwell and board time together.

      Select a station to inspect its board, or choose Start here to drive from that station. Reverse route starts a run toward the other terminus. In heading-up mode the train always faces up, including on the reverse route.

      The board reproduces the blue SBB Perronanzeiger style with German delay wording, scheduled departures, intermediate stops and sectors. The run uses a repeating sample timetable and simulated delays. Platform numbers follow the ZVV S8 platform reference. The map uses 2D BAV railway kilometer axes. Lake outline, platform lengths, track offsets and train operation are schematic.

      Starting a new run clears the current stop score.
